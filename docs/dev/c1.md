Deletion is only useful if the files are actively confusing the current work. Even then, I would prefer:
- archive them first
- keep one short summary of why they were abandoned
- then hide them from the main path

The only case where full deletion makes sense is if:
- they are junk duplicates,
- misleading unfinished drafts,
- or broken artifacts that actively contaminate the current thesis materials.

For research history, complete deletion is usually a mistake.

- **delete from the thesis story**
- **archive from the active workflow**
