
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

GibbsQ organizes the heterogeneous-routing problem into three layers with clearly separated claim strength. At the certified layer, we prove positive Harris recurrence for raw softmax and UAS via self-contained Foster--Lyapunov arguments, with explicit drift constants and temperature-independent certification for UAS. At the empirical closed-form layer, Calibrated UAS improves on the strongest classical baselines, but its benchmark-default setting remains outside the currently certified region. At the learned-policy layer, performance depends strongly on the teacher used for behavior cloning.

The main practical message is correspondingly simple: UAS is the principal certified routing law, Calibrated UAS is the strongest evaluated closed-form empirical extension, and the calibrated-teacher neural variant provides the best observed Protocol~B point estimate. The certified subset inside the calibrated family matters because it marks the part of that extension already covered by theorem-level analysis without overstating what has been proven.

Two limitations define the current boundary of the paper. First, the competitive benchmark-default calibrated parameters are not certified by the present sufficient condition, even though a numerically verified piecewise Lyapunov candidate suggests a plausible route to a stronger proof. Second, the strongest neural result is based on best-of-5 seed selection under Protocol~B and therefore should be read as directional rather than as an unbiased expected-performance estimate. All results also assume Markovian (Poisson/exponential) queueing dynamics.

The clearest next step is to extend certification from the current certified subset to practically competitive calibrated parameterizations. On the empirical side, the strongest conclusion already supported by the evidence is that analytical teachers matter more than the tested neural architecture tweaks: the 1.32-unit gap between calibrated-teacher and UAS-teacher initialization exceeds all tested architectural modifications combined. That connection between provable analytical structure and effective learned routing is the paper's central contribution.
